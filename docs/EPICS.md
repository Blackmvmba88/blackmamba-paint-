# BlackMamba Paint — EPIC Map

## EPIC 00 — Foundation
Rust workspace, stable IDs, events, serialization, logging, tests, CI, provider boundaries.

## EPIC 01 — Document Engine
`.bmpaint` document model for canvases, layers, strokes, cameras, references, timelines, knowledge and history.

## EPIC 02 — Infinite Spatial Engine
Sparse world coordinates, viewport, pan/zoom/rotation, spatial index, culling, LOD and tile cache.

## EPIC 03 — Stroke Engine
Pressure/tilt/time-aware strokes from mouse, stylus and touch.

## EPIC 04 — Path Editing
Nodes, Bézier handles, adaptive density, add/remove, simplify, subdivide, divide and join.

## EPIC 05 — Brush Engine
Separate stroke geometry from rendering behavior so brushes remain editable and replaceable.

## EPIC 06 — Layers + Object Graph
Raster, vector, stroke, reference, guide, animation and later lighting/material/3D layers with object relationships.

## EPIC 07 — TracePulse
Pixel magnifier, seed selection, local contour tracking, path extraction to a new layer, editing and animated reveal from the selected point.

## EPIC 08 — Time Native
Preserve stroke timing and direction as source data; replay, reverse, from-click and from-center reveal.

## EPIC 09 — Animation Studio
Timeline, keyframes, transforms, layer opacity, masks, stroke reveal and video render path.

## EPIC 10 — Camera System
Camera as first-class data across infinite canvas, animation, 360, sphere and future 3D.

## EPIC 11 — Perspective Core
1/2/3-point, curvilinear, fisheye, cylindrical, spherical, orthographic/isometric models with manual, assisted and automatic inference.

## EPIC 12 — Perspective Tutor
Teach perspective using live errors, hints, construction, solutions and generated exercises.

## EPIC 13 — Projection Canvas
Flat, infinite, cylindrical 360, full sphere and custom projection document modes.

## EPIC 14 — Perception Engine
Edges, shapes, planes, values, colors, depth, perspective, materials and composition analysis.

## EPIC 15 — Ghost Mentor
Non-destructive visual proposals over the artist's own work.

## EPIC 16 — Critique Lens
Impact-ranked critique with explicit `do not touch` protections.

## EPIC 17 — Visual Diagnostics
Grayscale, value reduction, blur, silhouette, edge map, saturation, temperature, mirror, thumbnail, focus map and X-Ray views.

## EPIC 18 — Material Brain
Visual behavior of metal, skin, glass, cloth, stone, plastics, wear, translucency, roughness and scale.

## EPIC 19 — Color Intelligence
Theory, hierarchy, temperature, saturation, psychology/marketing, fashion and cultural context.

## EPIC 20 — Reference Intelligence
References with explicit roles such as pose, color, material, clothing, lighting, composition and architecture.

## EPIC 21 — Art Knowledge Graph
Connected knowledge for art history, movements, artists, anatomy, architecture, photography, design, materials, symbols and concept art.

## EPIC 22 — Style Genome
Represent style as measurable relationships rather than only labels.

## EPIC 23 — Learning Engine
Skill graph, progress, mistakes, exercises and mastery.

## EPIC 24 — Challenge Generator
Generate targeted exercises from observed weaknesses.

## EPIC 25 — Artist Replay
Analyze how decisions were made, not only the final image.

## EPIC 26 — Visual History
Branching decision history instead of destructive linear experimentation.

## EPIC 27 — World Bible
Project-specific rules for physics, scale, color, materials, architecture, technology, symbolism and narrative constraints.

## EPIC 28 — Continuity Engine
Keep characters, props, palettes, insignia, materials and world rules coherent across multiple artworks.

## EPIC 29 — 3D Bridge
Blender interoperability, camera sync, curves/planes, paintovers and geometry/material hints.

## EPIC 30 — Spatial Art
Immersive spherical, room-scale, VR and interactive visual work.

## AI is cross-cutting

AI is not one epic near the end. From EPIC 00 onward it has a stable observation/proposal channel into the document. Capability increases as geometry, perspective, references, history and knowledge become available.
